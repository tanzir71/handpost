Before the product pitch

Relay is powerful.
It is not risk-free.

Relay publishes by driving real platform web interfaces. That avoids API rent and approval gates—but trades them for policy risk and ongoing maintenance. Install only if that trade makes sense for you.

01 / TERMS

Your account can be challenged—or banned.

Automated posting through web UIs violates most platforms’ terms, even at human volume. Circuit breakers, pacing, and caps reduce risk. They cannot remove it.

Read the live-testing policy ↗
02 / DRIFT

Selectors will break. Budget for repairs.

Platform interfaces change. Fixtures and per-platform selector modules localize each repair, but expect roughly one adapter repair per month.

Inspect the adapters ↗
03 / FALLBACK

Instagram and TikTok remain fragile.

When reliable automation is not possible, Relay prepares the content, opens the real composer, and hands control back to you. Manual fallback is a feature, not a hidden failure.

Straight answers

Before Relay touches a queue.

Will my account get banned?+

It is possible. Relay’s global concurrency, human pacing, daily caps, cooldowns, detection circuit breaker, and manual fallback reduce the likelihood and blast radius. None of them can make prohibited automation safe or approved.

Does Relay see my password or 2FA code?+

No. Connect opens a visible Chrome profile. You complete login and 2FA directly on the platform. Relay stores the profile directory locally; it never exports cookies to SQLite or logs.

Why not use official APIs?+

Official APIs are the safer path when their pricing, access, and feature scope work for you. Relay exists for the gap: a personal, low-volume publisher that does not want per-channel SaaS rent or multiple developer-app approval flows.

What happens when a platform changes its UI?+

The adapter fails closed, captures sanitized evidence, and offers manual completion. Per-platform selectors and saved fixtures keep the repair localized, but maintenance is expected.

Does the demo post anything?+

Never. It is a browser-only simulation with invented content. The timeline, permalink, and proof thumbnail are fake and clearly labeled.
