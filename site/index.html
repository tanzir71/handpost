Your posts, delivered by hand.

Handpost is a local-first desktop scheduler. It publishes through your own logged-in Chrome — and takes new posts from Claude, Codex or any script as plain files dropped in a folder.

How it works

From draft to receipt, without renting an API.

  1. 01

    Write it — or let Claude

    Compose in the app, or let a Claude scheduled task, Codex or any script drop a Markdown or JSON file into ~/Handpost/inbox.

  2. 02

    Handpost checks it

    Length, media and alt text are checked against every platform before anything opens. Then the post waits for your approval or takes the next free slot, paced and capped.

  3. 03

    Posted from your Chrome

    At its time, Handpost drives your own logged-in Chrome profile — one post at a time, at a human pace. You log in yourself; it never sees your password.

  4. 04

    You get a receipt

    Every post ends with a permalink and a proof screenshot — or an honest “needs you”, with the composer opened and your text copied. Writers read the same receipt.

Automation inbox

Claude writes. Your computer posts.

Handpost never calls an AI, and no AI ever touches a platform. The only interface is a folder: a writer drops one file per post into inbox/, Handpost checks it, schedules it and publishes it from your Chrome, then leaves a receipt the next run can read.

  • Plain files. Markdown with front matter, or JSON. No API, no server, no tokens to leak.
  • Atomic. Write to .tmp, rename into place — half-written files are never read.
  • Idempotent. Every post carries an id. The same file twice is a duplicate, never a double post.
  • Receipts and status. receipts/<id>.json and handpost.status.json tell the writer what happened and which slots are free.
  • Same guardrails. Inbox posts get the validation, pacing, caps and circuit breaker that hand-written posts get. Auto-schedule starts off.

Local-first

Nothing leaves your computer.

There is no Handpost server to send your drafts to. The database, your media, the browser sessions and every proof screenshot live in folders on your disk — back them up, export them, or delete them. The app itself makes no network calls: no telemetry, no update pings, no fonts from a CDN.

Honest caveats

Powerful. Not risk-free.

Handpost posts by driving the same web pages you use by hand. That avoids API fees and approval queues — and trades them for policy risk and upkeep. Install it only if that trade makes sense for you.

01 / Terms

Your account can be challenged — or banned.

Automated posting through web interfaces violates most platforms’ terms, even at human volume. A challenge, an action block or account loss is possible. Pacing, daily caps and the circuit breaker reduce the risk; they cannot remove it.

Read the live-test policy

02 / Drift

Platforms change. Selectors break.

When a composer changes, the adapter fails closed, saves evidence and hands the post back to you. Each repair stays inside one platform’s folder, but budget for roughly one adapter repair per month.

Inspect the adapters

03 / Fallback

Instagram and TikTok remain fragile.

When reliable automation isn’t possible, Handpost opens the real composer, copies your text and hands control back to you. Manual fallback is a feature, not a hidden failure.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Will my account get banned?

It’s possible. One post at a time, human pacing, daily caps, cooldowns, a circuit breaker that freezes a platform on any challenge, and manual fallback all lower the odds and the damage. None of them make automation approved by the platform.

Does Handpost see my password or 2FA codes?

No. Connecting an account opens a visible Chrome window and you log in on the platform’s own page. Handpost keeps that browser profile on your disk and never copies cookies into its database or logs.

Does Claude post for me?

No. Claude, Codex or a script only writes a file. Handpost validates it, paces it and publishes it — and with auto-schedule off, nothing goes out until you press Approve.

Why not use the official APIs?

When the pricing, access and features work for you, official APIs are the safer path. Handpost is for the gap: one person, low volume, no per-channel subscription and no developer-app approvals.

What happens when a platform changes its design?

The adapter stops instead of guessing, saves a sanitized snapshot of the page and offers to finish the post by hand. The fix is usually a selector or two in one platform’s folder.

What do I need to run it?

Windows, macOS or Linux, and Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. Installers arrive with the first release; until then it runs from source with Node.js 22 — see the README on GitHub.
